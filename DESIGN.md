## Brand & Style

This design system blends Czech pub heritage with contemporary culinary craft. Rooted in Žďár nad Sázavou's regional hospitality, it bridges traditional wood-and-copper brewpub coziness with streamlined, modern gastro-lounge polish. 

### Core Aesthetic Pillars
- **Crafted Warmth:** Rich ember tones, deep charred woods, and aged brass elements set against clean, breathable parchment tones.
- **Gastronomic Precision:** Editorial menu hierarchies with distinct weight measures, provenance details, and pricing clarity.
- **Contemporary Rustic:** Tactical mix of tactile organic surfaces, fine-gauge borders, and deliberate typographic rhythm instead of cluttered faux-vintage ornaments.

### Emotional Target
The user should experience a feeling of warmth, hearty hospitality, and unhurried culinary confidence—evoking the sizzle of an open-fire grill and the creamy pour of freshly tapped pilsner.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the brew-kettle, the charcoal grill, and natural table linens. 

### Palette Application
- **Primary (`#D97706` Amber):** Reserved for high-value interactive drivers: table reservations, key category badges, and highlighted chef specials.
- **Secondary (`#292524` Warm Charcoal):** Structural foundation for primary text, solid buttons, dark mode card containers, and footer bands.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C` Ember):** Micro-accents denoting grill signatures, hot specialties, and urgent updates (e.g., job postings, today's daily lunch deals).
- **Backgrounds (`#FAFAF9` & `#F5F5F4`):** Warm, parchment-like canvas providing maximum legibility for menu reading without the sterile glare of pure white.
- **Contrast & Hierarchy:** Inverted surfaces (such as evening menus, beer cellars, or operational hours) transition to rich `#1C1917` with typography set in `#FFFBEB` and `#E7E5E4`.

## Typography

Typographic scale is structured around editorial hospitality.

- **Headlines (Vollkorn):** Conveys historic Bohemian brewing roots with sturdy serifs, warm curves, and reliable weight. Used for restaurant naming, food category divisions, and seasonal story blocks.
- **Body Text (Manrope):** Delivers clean Scandinavian clarity to ingredient breakdowns, descriptions, and operational legalities.
- **Data, Pricing, and Portions (Space Grotesk):** Provides technical alignment for portion quantities (`100 g`, `0.5 l`), numeric pricing (`Kč`), and live operational schedules. The slight geometric edge contrasts organically with the literary warmth of Vollkorn.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a unified 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum container width of `1280px`.

### Responsive Rules
- **Mobile (&lt; 768px):** 4-column layout. Margins tighten to `1rem`. Menu navigation transforms into a sticky horizontal category carousel with pill chips to allow quick jumps across categories (Předkrmy, Steaky, Pivo). Menu items render single-column with title, portion, description, and price neatly aligned.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Split sections allow category filters on an anchored sticky sidebar with items stacking in 2-column masonry grids.
- **Desktop (&gt; 1024px):** 12-column system. 2-column menus mirror traditional physical print menus, pairing food items with curated beer pairings and direct pricing columns.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows and floating plastic card patterns, favoring tactile layer stacks and paper-cut depth reminiscent of fine table settings.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#FAFAF9` parchment ground.
2. **Level 1 (Menu Plate & Surface Cards):** `#F5F5F4` surface encased with an organic thin border: `1px solid #E7E5E4`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Modals & Hover States):** Tonal elevation via dark warm charcoal tint: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Dark Anchor Surfaces / Bar Highlights):** Deep `#1C1917` tone with inner amber glow `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)` to create an illuminated evening atmosphere.

## Shapes

The design uses a soft, deliberate `0.25rem` (4px) baseline radius for structural cards, plates, and interactive components. This gives elements a structured, architectural feel reminiscent of traditional woodcraft and printed paper menus. Small tags, status chips, and promotional pills utilize `rounded-full` boundaries to create a clear functional distinction between structure and active state tags.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary (Action/Reservation):** Background `#D97706`, text `#1C1917`, font `Space Grotesk` medium, radius `4px`. Hover: transitions to `#B45309` with text `#FFFBEB`.
- **Secondary (Beer/Bar Links):** Background `#292524`, text `#FAFAF9`, hover: `#1C1917` with subtle amber outline.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E7E5E4`, text `#292524`. Hover: background `#F5F5F4`.

### 2. Category Anchor Chips
- Horizontal scrolling pill container for rapid section jumping (`Polévky`, `Steaky`, `Dezerty`).
- Default: `#F5F5F4` background, border `1px solid #E7E5E4`, text `#44403C`.
- Active: `#292524` background, text `#FAFAF9`, border `1px solid #292524`.

### 3. Menu Item Row / Card
- **Layout:** Dot leader or flex space-between row connecting item title to price.
- **Portion Tag:** Space Grotesk `13px` medium in `#78716C` prefixed directly to the title (e.g., `100 g`, `0.5 l`).
- **Title:** Vollkorn `18px` semibold `#1C1917`.
- **Description:** Manrope `14px` regular `#57534E` nested underneath.
- **Price:** Space Grotesk `18px` bold `#B45309` right-aligned for instant eye scanning.

### 4. Schedule & Info Badges
- Compact tables featuring daily operating hours. Active day highlighted with a soft ember pill border (`#EA580C`) and `#FFFBEB` background.
- Special announcement tags (such as `Hledáme posilu`) use `#EA580C` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### 5. Input Fields
- Understated craft look: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #D6D3D1`. Focused: `1.5px solid #D97706` with no generic browser blue ring. Label positioned using `Space Grotesk` uppercase tracking.
